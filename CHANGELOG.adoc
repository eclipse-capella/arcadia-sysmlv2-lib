=== Breaking changes

- https://github.com/eclipse-capella/arcadia-sysmlv2-lib/issues/63[#63] The `portDirection` attribute of `ComponentPort` is removed: the direction of a component port is given by its directed features (`in [0..*] : ExchangeItem;`, `out [0..*] : ExchangeItem;`). A port delegation is expressed by a binding connector, and the kind of a component exchange is not translated (https://github.com/eclipse-capella/arcadia-sysmlv2-lib/issues/34[#34])
